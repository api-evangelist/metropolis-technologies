The Recognition Economy is no longer a theoretical concept — it’s live, scaling and expanding into new industries, right now. What started in parking — one of the most universal, high-frequency, transactional experiences in the physical world — is now the blueprint for how identity powers seamless, connected experiences everywhere. We’re moving into a future where presence is the interface, and recognition is infrastructure.
