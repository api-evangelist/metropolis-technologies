We’re working to eliminate a world filled with repetition, but this news is something we’re honored to be a part of (again). Metropolis has been named to CNBC's 2026 Disruptor 50 list , the fourteenth edition of the annual ranking celebrating the most innovative private companies reshaping the global economy. It's our second consecutive year on the list — and a reflection of what happens when a bold idea meets real-world execution.
