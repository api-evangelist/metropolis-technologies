The Metropolis Network is continuing to grow, and getting more valuable with every new Member. Across 4,500+ locations in North America, Metropolis now serves more than 30M Members, and that number continues to grow by 1M new Members every month. To put that growth in context: 1M new Members a month equates to about the population of San Jose, California or Austin, Texas joining us every 30 days.
