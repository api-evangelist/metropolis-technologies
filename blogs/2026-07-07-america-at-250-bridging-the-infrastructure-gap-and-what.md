After 250 years of American innovation, the next frontier is recognition Two hundred and fifty years ago, the United States of America was founded on new ideas and innovation. Over the past two and a half centuries, that innovation reshaped how people move — from horse and carriage to the Model T, steam locomotives to high-speed rail, propeller planes to commercial jets, combustion engines to electric and autonomous vehicles. The machines that move people transformed, generation after generation.
