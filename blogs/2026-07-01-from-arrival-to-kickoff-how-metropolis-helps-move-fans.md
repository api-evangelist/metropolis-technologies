The 2026 World Cup is the largest in the tournament’s history, spanning three countries and 16 cities in North America. Every successful match day depends on an invisible network of mobility infrastructure — parking, airports, transit systems, shuttles, security. Metropolis is supporting the movement of millions of fans through host cities, drawing on decades of experience managing complex transportation, parking and event operations at some of the world’s largest live events, including the Super Bowl .
