When San Antonio International Airport (SAT) realized that legacy airport parking systems were no longer meeting the demands of the modern traveler — and constraining airport growth and revenue — they decided to make a bold move. The Texas hub serves more than 11 million passengers annually and partnered with Metropolis to replace antiquated hardware and analog systems with computer vision-powered recognition technology. According to Jennifer Pysher, Assistant Aviation Director at SAT, the transition moved SAT "light years ahead” of competition.
