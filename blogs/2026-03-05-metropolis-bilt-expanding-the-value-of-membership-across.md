A look back at four months of the Bilt + Metropolis partnership When we announced our partnership with Bilt back in October , our goal was — and still is — to reshape how people move through and interact with the real world. Bilt is a payments and rewards program that enables renters and homeowners to earn points on everyday expenses and redeem them for travel, dining, fitness and rent. As Bilt’s exclusive parking partner, Metropolis now provides a way for our Members to earn those same rewards simply by parking.
