How Metropolis delivered seamless mobility and access across one of the world’s most demanding environments Super Bowls are among the most operationally complex live events in the world. For Super Bowl LX, that complexity extended beyond Levi’s Stadium to the entire Bay Area. More than 135 million people watched in the United States while hundreds of thousands moved across the Bay Area over the course of the week, including fans, league executives, media, sponsors, performers and members of both the Seattle Seahawks and New England Patriots organizations and families.
